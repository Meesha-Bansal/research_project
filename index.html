How to use

Your guide to
exploring biology

Everything you need to know to get the most out of Unfold Biology — from spinning a 3D heart to asking your AI tutor tricky exam questions.

01
🫀
Pick a Module

Choose Circulatory System or Neural Control from the cards above. Each one loads a 3D scene built for your chapter.

02
🖱️
Navigate the 3D Model

Click + drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. See every angle of the structure.

03
🏷️
Click Labels to Learn

Tap any label on the model to open its description, function, and key facts like a textbook that responds to you.

04
💬
Ask the AI Tutor

Click Ask AI Tutor in the top bar. The panel slides in on the right ask questions while looking at the model.

05
🔁
Switch Modules

Come back to this home page to switch topics. Your AI Tutor chat stays active across modules in the same tab.

💡
Pro Tips for Students
→
Rotate the model fully — some labelled parts are hidden on the back side.
→
After clicking a label, ask the AI Tutor to explain it in more detail.
→
Use the AI Tutor to compare both modules for exam prep — e.g. neurons vs cardiac muscle.
